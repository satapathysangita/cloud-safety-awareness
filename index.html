Stay Safe in a Cloud-Driven World

Simple steps to protect yourself online and understand cloud safety.

What is Cloud Safety?

Cloud safety means protecting your personal data when using online services. It includes understanding how your information is stored, shared, and secured across cloud platforms.

Online Safety Tips

  • Use strong, unique passwords.
  • Enable multi-factor authentication.
  • Avoid clicking suspicious links.
  • Check website security (look for HTTPS).
  • Be careful with what you share online.

Cloud Safety Basics

Cloud services store your data on remote servers. To stay safe:

  • Use trusted cloud providers.
  • Review privacy settings regularly.
  • Back up important files.
  • Use secure networks when accessing cloud apps.
